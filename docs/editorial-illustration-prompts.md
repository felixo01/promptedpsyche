## Trust: hidden sources

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: one original editorial cover illustration for an essay about trust in ready-made AI answers. Landscape 3:2 aspect ratio.

Primary request: A confident, clean speech-bubble-shaped sheet of paper has one corner lifted, exposing a dense, uneven set of layered source fragments underneath. The neat synthesized answer hides a complicated provenance: conflicting evidence, uncertainty, and different perspectives. A small terracotta thread winds through the exposed fragments and back toward the lifted corner, making the possibility of reconstructing the answer's origins visible. One strong visual metaphor, clear at thumbnail size; not a literal flowchart.

Composition: a large, simple paper answer silhouette above a compact irregular mass of torn source fragments, with the fold and exposed underside immediately legible. Use just a few purposeful shapes and spacious off-white margins. The sources differ through their shapes and the directions of their carved marks. The upper answer surface is calm and orderly; the revealed layers are visibly heterogeneous. No hands are needed. No desk, room, books, or decorative still life.

Style and medium: sophisticated conceptual print illustration combining linocut or woodcut linework with cut-paper collage. Bold, considered silhouettes; tactile ink, restrained carved hatching and slight print irregularity. Flat graphic printmaking, not realistic photography or 3D. The fold is described by shape and carved linework, not a soft rendered shadow.

Palette: off-white #FAFAF8, charcoal #242026, deep plum #4D284C, and a very sparse terracotta #B3412B accent reserved for the provenance thread.

Text: none. Marks are abstract ink hatching, not letters, numbers, pseudo-writing, charts, or data.
Avoid: photography, lifestyle or stock imagery, book still lifes, gradients, soft 3D corporate blobs, brains, robots, chips, glowing screens, logos, watermarks, legible text, fake data, decorative graphs, arrows, and flowchart nodes.
```
